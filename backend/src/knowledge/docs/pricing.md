# Pricing & Budget Ranges

## How We Price Projects

All Bunny Honey projects are custom-priced based on scope, complexity, and timeline. We don't have fixed packages because every client's needs are unique.

## Budget Ranges

- **Small Projects**: Under €1,000
  - Quick automations, simple chatbot integrations, small content workflows

- **Medium Projects**: €1,000 - €5,000
  - Multi-step automations, custom dashboards, AI content systems for single platforms

- **Large Projects**: €5,000 - €20,000
  - Complex YouTube automation, multi-platform content systems, custom SaaS MVPs

- **Enterprise Projects**: €20,000+
  - Full-scale AI solutions, ongoing retainers, comprehensive automation ecosystems

## What Affects Pricing

- Number of integrations (APIs, platforms, tools)
- Complexity of AI logic and workflows
- Custom design requirements
- Ongoing maintenance needs
- Timeline urgency

## Getting a Quote

To provide an accurate quote, we need to understand:
1. What type of project you have in mind
2. Your approximate budget range
3. Timeline expectations
4. Key features or integrations needed

Contact us to discuss your specific project and get a detailed proposal.
