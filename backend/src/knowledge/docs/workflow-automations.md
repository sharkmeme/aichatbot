# Workflow Automations

## What We Automate

Back-office operations, lead generation, outreach, and internal processes using n8n and custom integrations.

## Common Automation Solutions

### Lead Generation & Management
- Lead collection from multiple sources
- Automated lead scoring and qualification
- CRM integrations and data synchronization
- Lead routing to sales teams

### Outreach Automation
- LinkedIn and email outreach campaigns
- Follow-up sequences based on engagement
- Personalized messaging at scale
- Response tracking and analytics

### HR & Recruiting
- Telegram bots for candidate screening
- Automated onboarding workflows
- Interview scheduling automation
- Document collection and processing

### Reporting & Analytics
- Automated data collection from multiple tools
- Custom dashboard generation
- Scheduled reports via email or Slack
- Real-time performance monitoring

## Technology Stack

We primarily use n8n for workflow automation, which allows for:
- Easy maintenance and modifications
- Visual workflow editing
- Hundreds of pre-built integrations
- Self-hosted or cloud deployment
